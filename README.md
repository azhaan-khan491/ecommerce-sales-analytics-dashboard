# 📊 E-Commerce Sales Analytics Dashboard

## 🚀 Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end Data Analytics workflow using Excel, SQL, Python, and Power BI. The objective was to analyze retail sales data, identify business trends, evaluate product profitability, and create interactive dashboards for decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Identify top-performing customers and products
- Evaluate regional and state-wise sales trends
- Understand customer segment contributions
- Analyze discount impact on profitability
- Build interactive business dashboards

## 💡 Key Business Insights

### Customer Insights

- Consumer Segment generated the highest sales.
- Adrian Barton was the highest revenue-generating customer.

### Geographic Insights

- California generated the highest sales.
- New York City generated the highest revenue among cities.

### Product Insights

- Technology was the most profitable category.
- Several products generated significant losses despite strong sales performance.

### Profitability Insights

- Discounts above 20% significantly reduced profitability.
- Higher discounts were directly associated with increased losses.
